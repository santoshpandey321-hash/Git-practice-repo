Lab 1

Your First DAG on MWAA

Provision a managed Airflow environment and the full data pipeline with one command, then build and run your first DAG — ending with hands-on incident recovery.

  • One-shot setup: pipeline (Glue, S3, DynamoDB, Step Functions) + MWAA
  • Build a 3-task Glue pipeline DAG step by step
  • Incident ops: clear tasks, clear a DAG run, mark success

 Lab 2

Dataset-Driven DAGs

Split the pipeline across two DAGs that hand off through data, not a schedule — using Airflow 3's Asset (data-aware) scheduling to auto-trigger the consumer.

  • Producer DAG declares an Asset via outlets
  • Consumer DAG scheduled on that Asset — auto-triggered, no polling
  • Watch the hand-off and the Assets dependency graph
